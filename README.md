# 📊 Orbit Retail & Services — Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, identify business trends, and generate actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and revenue data for Orbit Retail & Services Pvt. Ltd. using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance across products, regions, and time periods, helping identify sales trends, profitability patterns, and potential areas for improvement.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Identify top-performing products by sales and profit.
- Compare regional sales and profitability.
- Evaluate discount strategies and their relationship with profit margins.
- Analyze transaction-level profitability.
- Compare quantity sold against net sales.
- Develop actionable business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Formulas and Functions
- Data Analysis and Visualization
- KPI Cards and Dashboard Design

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes the following analyses:

| Analysis | Description |
|---|---|
| Sales Performance | Tracks overall sales and year-over-year growth. |
| Product Performance | Compares products by net sales, profit, and profit margin. |
| Regional Analysis | Evaluates sales and profit across five regions. |
| Discount Analysis | Examines discounted versus non-discounted transactions. |
| Profitability Analysis | Calculates reported profit margins and checks for negative profit values. |
| Quantity vs. Revenue | Compares product sales volumes with net sales. |

## ⚠️ Data Limitation

The reported profit figures are based on the existing Profit column in the dataset. As a separate cost column is not available, actual profitability and losses could not be independently verified.
